== v1.0.0 (2026-10-11)

=== First release of Stra-ParlaBO

Stra-ParlaBO collects conversations and semi-structured interviews recorded in Bologna and its province, involving speakers of Italian as a foreign or second language. It was compiled within the "DiverSIta – Diversity in spoken Italian" project (PNRR, NextGenerationEU; PI: Caterina Mauri).

* 84 conversations with 138 speakers, anonymized; collection year 2025
* Conversation metadata include the `community` of the speakers (moroccan, bengali, chinese, ukrainian)
* Some conversations have a `_trad` tier with an Italian translation of the tokens produced in the speakers' other languages
* Time-aligned ELAN files, verticalized `tsv/` files (20 columns, including the `code_variation` features), linearized `linear-enriched/` and `linear-orthographic/` texts, and CSVW metadata (`csv-metadata.json`)
