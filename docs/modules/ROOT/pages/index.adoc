= Stra-ParlaBO

Stra-ParlaBO is part of the xref:portal::index.adoc[KIParla collection]. It consists of
conversations and semi-structured interviews collected in Bologna and its province,
involving speakers of Italian as a foreign or second language. Some conversations include
an Italian translation of tokens originally produced in the speaker's other language(s).

The module is made up of 84 conversations and 138 speakers. Transcriptions are anonymized.

The corpus was compiled within the "DiverSIta – Diversity in spoken Italian" project (PI: Caterina Mauri),
funded by the European Union – NextGenerationEU under the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR) –
Mission 4 Education and research (MUR project code P2022RFR8T, CUP J53D23017320001).

For the repository layout, the metadata, the verticalized format and the translations, see the
https://github.com/KIParla/Stra-ParlaBO#readme[README]. The module's differences from the
shared conventions are listed in xref:transcription-conventions.adoc[Transcription conventions].
