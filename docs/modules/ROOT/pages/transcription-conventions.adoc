= Transcription conventions — Stra-ParlaBO
:guidelines-version: 1.0

Stra-ParlaBO follows the xref:portal:transcription-conventions.adoc[KIParla
transcription conventions], version {guidelines-version}.

The sections below are the *only* points where Stra-ParlaBO differs from or adds
to the shared conventions. Anything not listed here follows the shared page
unchanged.

== Extensions

Symbols or uses that the shared conventions do not define, added by Stra-ParlaBO.

=== `#_` later in the unit — rest of the unit in another language

Shared: `#_` at the start of a unit marks the whole unit as non-Italian
(see xref:portal:transcription-conventions.adoc#language[Language variation and code-switching]).
In Stra-ParlaBO `#_` may also appear *later* in a unit: everything from the marker to the end of
the unit is in a code other than Italian, so the unit can start in Italian and switch.

`#_` is written as a separate item followed by a space. When an overlap bracket opens at the same
point, write `#_ [` — not `[#_`. The pipeline rewrites `#_word`, `word#_` and `[#_` accordingly.

In the derived data every token after the marker gets `Variety=Other` and
`Language=NO_ISO_CODE`. Pauses, non-verbal behaviour, anonymized names and `xxx` after the marker
are not marked as non-Italian.

=== `#*word` — doubtful non-Italian token

Placed before a single token that is *possibly* non-Italian or dialectal, when the transcriber is
unsure. Derived as `Variety=Unassignable`.

=== `$word` — emerging or non-standard form

Placed before a single token to mark a non-standard or emerging form. It is not code-switching.
Derived as `Nonce=Yes` (a feature of the token, not a variety).

=== `# ` at the start of a unit — unattributed code-switching

A `#` followed by a space at the very start of a unit marks that the unit contains code-switching
which cannot be attributed to specific words.
Derived as `Variety=Unsure` on every token of the unit that has no marker of its own.

=== `<speaker>_trad` — translation tiers

A child tier named `<speaker>_trad` (for example `PSB033_trad`) carries an Italian translation of that
speaker's foreign-language speech. These tiers are not tokenized: they are collected in the
`translations/` folder of the repository.

=== `suoni` tier

Non-speech sounds and music that are not part of any speaker's turn (for example `((musica))`)
are reported on a `suoni` tier rather than on a speaker tier. It is not a participant tier.

== Overrides

Cases where Stra-ParlaBO applies a different or stricter rule than the shared conventions.

=== Music and inserts tier name

Overrides xref:portal:transcription-conventions.adoc#nvb[Non-verbal behaviour, noise and music]:
the dedicated tier for music and similar inserts is called `suoni`, not `musica`.
